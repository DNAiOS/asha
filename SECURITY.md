# Security

Report a vulnerability in the Asha API, the `asha` CLI, the MCP server or this repository privately, through GitHub: **Security → Report a vulnerability** on this repository. If you cannot use GitHub, email hello@dnai.systems with "security" in the subject.

Please include the endpoint or file, the steps to reproduce, and any A2A task id. Do not include patient data or real health information in a report.

We acknowledge reports within 3 business days and tell you what we will fix and when. Please give us a reasonable window to fix the issue before you disclose it.

Out of scope: denial-of-service testing, automated scanning that sends high volumes of requests, and social engineering of DNAi staff. Test only against your own key and stay inside its rate limits.
